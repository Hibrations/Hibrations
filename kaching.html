Kaching

make · save · grow — one app for all three, built Denmark-first and personalised for Lek (DKK, dansk/English toggle, local rails).

Try it

  • Tap the bottom tabs to move between the 3 pillars.
  • Grow → "Rund op nu" to watch spare change invest.
  • Goals → "+150 kr" to close a ring.
  • Top-right DA/EN switches language live.

The thinking

One hero number (net worth). AI reads your data in plain language. Streaks reward consistency, never shame a bad day. Every game mechanic maps to real kroner — no empty points.
